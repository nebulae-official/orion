Orion Brand System · v1.0 · 6 Directions

Six marks.
One mission control.

Each direction is a distinct answer to the same brief: what does an autonomous content swarm look like, when compressed into a single glyph? All six share the nebula palette; they diverge in metaphor, geometry, and voice.

The brief

Orion runs a swarm of agents that turn trending signal into published content. The mark should feel like the control panel of a quiet, powerful machine.

  • Constellation of actors. Scout, Director, Media, Editor, Publisher — five specialized agents orbiting one human operator.
  • Signal, then synthesis. The product listens before it speaks; the mark should hint at capture and emission.
  • Precision over theatrics. Infrastructure tool tone — Linear / Palantir / Bloomberg, not SaaS-startup.
  • Works at 16px. Legible as a favicon, scales to 512px tile without losing soul.

Primary pick

D01 Aperture

The aperture best captures Orion's soul: a control-room viewport onto a constellation of agents. Reads at 16px, survives on dark and light, ownable without being cute.

Strong runners-up

D02 Hunter · D06 Prism

D02 wins if you want the "Orion" name taken literally — the constellation lives inside the O. D06 wins if you want the clearest visual metaphor for "one trend → many formats."
